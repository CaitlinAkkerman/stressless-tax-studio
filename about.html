My Story

Tax Help That Feels Like Talking to a Friend

I started Stress(Less) Tax Studio because your tax preparer should leave you with answers, not more questions. I want my clients to feel informed and empowered, not confused and exhausted.

About Me

Hi, I'm Caitlin

I've spent 5 years doing other people's taxes so they don't have to do all the math right and still end up confused about the result. I focus exclusively on personal returns — no LLCs, no board of directors, just you, your income, and the receipts you swear you kept somewhere. When I'm not sorting through chaos, you'll find me chasing adventure with my kids — on the road or on the water. Same human, same energy, different kind of mess.

I'm more than a tax preparer — I'm an actual human who remembers your name, your situation, and that you started that weird side hustle in February. Gig income, side hustle receipts, that heart-stopping "wait, do I owe more??" moment — I handle the mess so you don't have to relive it every April.

✦ Empathetic

I've heard every version of "I think I messed this up" and never once judged. Your tax situation isn't going to shock me.

✦ Clear

No jargon, no accountant-speak, no pretending Schedule C is self-explanatory. Just plain English and answers you actually understand.

✦ Approachable

Ask anything. There is never a dumb tax question. A little weird, maybe — but I've gotten weirder.

✦ Motivating

You'll leave feeling like you've got this — not just your taxes, but your finances in general.
